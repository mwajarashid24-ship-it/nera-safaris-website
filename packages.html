6 DaysSerengeti · Ngorongoro

Serengeti Untamed

Track the wildebeest columns across the northern Serengeti and descend into Ngorongoro Crater, one of the densest concentrations of wildlife on Earth.

Day 1
Arrive Kilimanjaro, transfer to Arusha. Meet your guide, gear check, briefing over dinner in Arusha.
Day 2–3
Central Serengeti. Game drives through Seronera, the park's richest predator territory.
Day 4
Northern Serengeti. Search out the migration herds and, in season, a Mara River crossing.
Day 5
Ngorongoro Crater. Full-day descent into the crater floor — dense wildlife in a compact area.
Day 6
Depart. Transfer back to Arusha / Kilimanjaro for your onward flight.

Included

  • Private 4x4 vehicle and driver-guide
  • All park and conservation fees
  • Full-board accommodation
  • Airstrip/road transfers

Not Included

  • International flights
  • Visa fees
  • Tips and gratuities
  • Personal travel insurance

Adventure
7 DaysKilimanjaro · Machame

Kilimanjaro Conquest

The Machame route with an extra acclimatization day built in — private porters, cook, and a guide certified for high-altitude ascents.

Day 1
Machame Gate → Machame Camp. Enter through rainforest, gentle first day to acclimatize.
Day 2
Machame → Shira Camp. Climb into moorland with your first full views of the summit.
Day 3
Shira → Barranco (via Lava Tower). Climb high, sleep low — a key acclimatization day.
Day 4
Barranco → Karanga Camp. The Barranco Wall scramble, then a short afternoon.
Day 5
Karanga → Barafu Camp. Short trekking day, early rest before the summit push.
Day 6
Summit via Stella Point → Mweka Camp. Midnight ascent to Uhuru Peak, then descend to Mweka.
Day 7
Mweka → Gate → Arusha. Final descent, certificates, transfer back to town.

Included

  • Certified summit guide + support crew
  • Porters, cook, park & camping fees
  • All meals on the mountain
  • Pre-climb briefing and gear check

Not Included

  • Personal climbing gear rental
  • Tips for the mountain crew
  • Post-climb hotel nights
  • Travel/rescue insurance
$1,890 / per person

6 DaysRuaha National Park

Ruaha Wild Escape

Tanzania's largest park and one of its quietest — baobab-studded plains, huge elephant herds, and some of the best predator sightings in the country.

Day 1
Fly to Ruaha. Light aircraft transfer, afternoon game drive on arrival.
Day 2–4
Ruaha game drives. Full days tracking predators along the Great Ruaha River.
Day 5
Walking safari. Guided bush walk with an armed ranger, tracking on foot.
Day 6
Depart. Final morning drive, fly back out.

Included

  • Light aircraft transfers
  • Private vehicle and guide
  • Walking safari with armed ranger
  • Full-board tented camp

Not Included

  • International flights
  • Visa fees
  • Tips
  • Alcoholic drinks at camp

1 DayDar es Salaam

Dar es Salaam City Tour

A full day through Tanzania's largest city — the bustle of Kariakoo market, the National Museum, Kivukoni fish market at dawn, and a sunset stop at the Msasani Slipway.

Morning
Kivukoni Fish Market & Kariakoo. Watch the daily fish auction, then browse the stalls and spice sellers of Kariakoo market.
Midday
National Museum & City Centre. Tanzania's history from the Olduvai fossils to independence, followed by a walk past the Askari Monument and St. Joseph's Cathedral.
Afternoon
Msasani Peninsula. Coastal drive out to Msasani, with time to browse craft stalls at the Slipway.
Evening
Sunset at the Slipway. Waterfront dinner option overlooking the Indian Ocean before drop-off.

Included

  • Private vehicle and English-speaking guide
  • Museum entry fees
  • Bottled water throughout the day
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off

Not Included

  • Lunch and dinner
  • Personal shopping
  • Tips

Food & Culture
1 DayDar es Salaam

Gastronomy Experience

A guided food journey through Dar es Salaam — street food stalls, a Swahili spice cooking class, and a seafood dinner by the water. For travelers who want to taste the country, not just see it.

Morning
Market & spice walk. Wander Kariakoo market with your guide, tasting fruit, snacks, and learning the spices behind Swahili cooking.
Midday
Cooking class. Hands-on Swahili cooking class — pilau, coconut fish curry, and chapati, eaten as your own lunch.
Afternoon
Street food crawl. Sample mishkaki, chips mayai, and fresh juice from local vendors around the city.
Evening
Seafood dinner. Close the day with fresh grilled seafood at a waterfront restaurant.

Included

  • Private guide and vehicle
  • All tastings, cooking class, and dinner
  • Recipe cards to take home
  • Bottled water throughout

Not Included

  • Alcoholic drinks
