About Nera Safaris

Run By Guides Who Grew Up On This Land

Nera Safaris was founded in Dar es Salaam by Rashid Mwaja, a lifelong traveler who has explored nearly every major city in Tanzania. Nera was built to share that same passion — small groups, honest pacing, and routes that respond to where the animals actually are, not a printed schedule.

  • Private vehicles only — never shared with strangers
  • Government-licensed guides, most bilingual in Swahili and English
  • Fair-share partnerships with Maasai and local community camps
