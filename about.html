Our Story

Founder-Led, From Dar es Salaam

Nera Safaris was founded by Rashid Mwaja, a lifelong traveler born and raised in Dar es Salaam who has explored nearly every major city in Tanzania — hiking, photographing, and filming the country's landscapes along the way. Rashid saw how larger operators tend to run the same fixed itineraries for every guest: printed months in advance, shared vehicles, guides briefed on a script rather than the land itself.

Nera was built to fix that. Every trip is led by a guide with a home base in the region you're visiting, so what you get isn't a script — it's a local's read on the day.

  • Private vehicles only — never shared with strangers
  • Government-licensed guides, most bilingual in Swahili and English
  • Fair-share partnerships with Maasai and local community camps
  • Itineraries adjusted in the field, not fixed on paper
What We Stand For

Three Things We Don't Compromise On

Local, Always

Every guide is Tanzanian, licensed, and guiding the region they grew up in — not rotated between parks they don't know.

Private By Default

Your vehicle is yours. We don't fill seats with strangers to cut costs, even on our lower-priced itineraries.

Fair-Share Tourism

We partner directly with Maasai-run and community-owned camps so more of what you spend stays in the region you're visiting.
